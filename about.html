About Botswana Talent Bridge

Your gateway to employment opportunities across Botswana

Our Purpose

Botswana Jobs Connect is designed to centralize job opportunities into one accessible platform. It helps job seekers easily discover, access, and apply for employment opportunities across Botswana.

Target Audience

  • Individuals aged 18–65
  • Job seekers within Botswana
  • Unemployed individuals, interns, and attachment seekers

What We Offer

The platform provides a simple and user-friendly way to browse job listings. Users can search and filter jobs using keywords and locations, making it easier to find relevant opportunities.

Technology Behind the System

This system is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Job data is stored and displayed dynamically, allowing users to search and filter results without requiring a backend server.
